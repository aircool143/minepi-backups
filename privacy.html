Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

MinePi Backups is a personal utility used by its owner to create and manage backups of a Minecraft server.

Information and access

The utility uses Google OAuth 2.0 to authorize access to Google Drive for storing and managing Minecraft backup archives. It may access files within the Google Drive location configured by the owner.

How information is used

Google Drive access is used only to upload, verify, list, and manage Minecraft backup archives. The utility is intended for personal use by the server owner.

Storage and sharing

Backup archives are stored on the owner's server and in the configured Google Drive location. The utility does not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

Data retention and deletion

The owner manages backup retention and may delete backup archives from local storage or Google Drive. Google account access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.

Security

Authentication credentials are used to connect to Google services. The owner is responsible for protecting the server and its credentials.

Contact

For questions about this application, contact the application owner through the support email listed on its Google consent screen.
